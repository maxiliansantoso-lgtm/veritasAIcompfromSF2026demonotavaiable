# Official Hackathon Guide — Notes

Source: **Veritas AI International High School Hackathon 2026 — Official Participant Guide**, uploaded in this project conversation.

## Event format

- Fully virtual and free; open to high school students worldwide.
- Individuals or teams of up to three.
- Hackathon dates: November 14–15, 2026 (ET).
- The guide lists live finals and awards on November 21, 2026.

## Required submission items

The guide says one person submits for the whole team and asks for:
1. Working code, as a Colab notebook or a GitHub repository anyone with the link can view.
2. A short write-up covering the problem, approach, results, and what did not work.
3. A demo video of up to two minutes, uploaded as an unlisted YouTube video or shared from Google Drive.
4. Names of all team members.
5. Citations for every dataset, model, and code source used.

## Suggested write-up structure

- Project name
- Track and team
- The problem
- What we built
- How it works (data and model)
- Results (baseline, model score on held-out test data, failures and likely causes)
- What we would do next
- Sources

## Judging criteria

The guide identifies four criteria:
- **Technical quality:** working project, sound approach, fair testing; compare against a baseline and report held-out results.
- **Impact:** who has the problem and what changes for them.
- **Creativity:** a fresh application or question.
- **Clarity:** understandable demo, concise write-up, and one main takeaway.

## Responsible AI and integrity

The guide says to build during the hackathon weekend, credit work that was not made by the team, and be honest about results. It specifically recommends reporting disappointing results, checking performance across groups where relevant, and explaining limitations.

## Important timing note

The uploaded guide says the hackathon ran November 14–15, 2026 (ET), and its submission section refers to a Sunday, November 15 deadline but the extracted text does not include the exact time. This repository publish does **not** submit anything to the hackathon portal.
